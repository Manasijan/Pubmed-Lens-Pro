# ⚡ PubMed-Lens Pro
**PubMed-Lens Pro** is a high-performance Tampermonkey userscript designed for clinicians, researchers, and academic surgeons. It transforms standard PubMed search results into an interactive, SaaS-like clinical evidence appraisal workstation with a clean dual-pane layout.
---
## 🚀 Key Features
* **⚖️ Oxford CEBM Evidence Levels**: Automatically classifies search results into Level 1 (SR/Meta-Analysis) through Level 5 (Narrative Review/Opinion) with distinct color coding.
* **📋 PICO Appraisal Cards**: Extracts real patient sample sizes ($N$), clinical regimens ($I/C$), and complete clinical outcomes directly from NCBI abstract XML in real time.
* **🏛️ Journal Rigor & Metrics**: Integrates NIH iCite citation counts, Relative Citation Ratios (RCR), Scimago Quartiles (Q1–Q4), and CAS Tiers.
* **🔗 Sister Trials Engine**: Instant 1-click modal querying of NCBI Entrez to locate high-rigor RCTs and systematic reviews on the same clinical topic.
* **🎛️ Interactive Multi-Select Filter Dashboard**: Filter papers in real time using evidence level chips, journal rigor tags, citation thresholds, and open-access toggles without losing page position.
* **📥 Advanced Synthesis Export**: Instantly export screened literature matrices to CSV (Excel/Covidence), formatted Microsoft Word `.doc` tables, or copy structured citations in **Vancouver, AMA, APA, MLA, Chicago, or IEEE** styles.
* **🔄 Infinite Pagination Support**: Seamlessly enhances new search results as you scroll or click "Show more results".
---
## 📦 Installation
1. Install the [Tampermonkey](https://www.tampermonkey.net/) extension for your browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari).
2. Click the Tampermonkey icon and select **Create a new script...**.
3. Copy the contents of `pubmed-lens.user.js` from this repository, paste it into the editor, and press `Ctrl + S` to save.
4. Navigate to any [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) search query and enjoy your upgraded workstation!
